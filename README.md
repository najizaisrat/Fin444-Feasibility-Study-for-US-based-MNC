# Feasibility Study: US-Based Furniture Plant
 
A FIN 444.1 (Spring 2026) group report from North South University comparing **China, Tanzania, Kazakhstan and Colombia** as locations for a wholly-owned US-based furniture manufacturing plant.
 
## Summary
 
The four countries are compared on 14 macroeconomic and regulatory factors (2021-2025), including GDP growth, inflation, interest rates, currency stability, reserves, banking strength and ease of doing business.
 
**Result: China is the most feasible location**, thanks to its stable economy, strong manufacturing base, large reserves and solid banking system. Tanzania, Kazakhstan and Colombia each have structural weaknesses (bureaucracy, commodity dependence, and trade deficits with high inflation, respectively).
